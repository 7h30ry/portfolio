About

I'm a cybersecurity engineer and analyst working across the full defensive and offensive stack: SIEM engineering, SOC operations and penetration testing. At TalixMan Group I design and operate Wazuh-based detection infrastructure, from custom decoders and rules to containerised log pipelines monitoring enterprise-critical systems.

My research background is in network security for resource-constrained environments. My final-year project built an SDN framework with unsupervised machine learning for real-time intrusion detection on IoT networks, and that work now drives my interest in in-network security measurement for converged IT/OT critical infrastructure.

Outside work I compete. With Team Error, representing Nigeria, I've reached the ECOWAS Regional Cybersecurity Hackathon podium four times, culminating in the 2026 championship in Accra.
